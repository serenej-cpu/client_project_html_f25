Career FAQ

  1. How do I write an effective resume? Tailor your resume to each job, use clear formatting, highlight relevant skills and experience, and keep it concise—generally one page for early-career professionals.
  2. What should I include in a cover letter? Address the specific position, explain why you’re interested and a good fit, and provide examples of your qualifications and achievements.
  3. How can I prepare for job interviews? Research the company, practice common interview questions, prepare examples of your experience, and have thoughtful questions ready for the interviewer.
  4. Where can I find job or internship opportunities? Use company career pages, job boards, networking platforms like LinkedIn, and your university’s career services or job portals.
  5. How can I negotiate a job offer? Research typical salaries for the role and location, clearly communicate your expectations, and be respectful and professional in discussions with potential employers.
